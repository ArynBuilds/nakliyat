# Yusufoğlu Nakliyat

> İstanbul merkezli, 2000'den bu yana faaliyet gösteren evden eve ve şehirlerarası nakliyat şirketi.

## Register

`brand`

The website IS the product. Its job is to convert anxious, comparison-shopping visitors into phone calls and WhatsApp leads. Design is not decoration — it is the entire selling apparatus.

## Users

**Primary**: Turkish residents in Istanbul (and intercity) preparing for a residential or commercial move. Often:
- Mobile-first (60–80% of traffic). Browsing in the evening after a long day.
- Stressed, anxious about logistics. Worried about damage, hidden costs, no-shows.
- Comparing 3–5 nakliyat companies side-by-side. Skeptical of templated sites.
- Decision triggers: trust, transparency, responsiveness, price clarity, evidence of real work.

**Secondary**: Office moves (kurumsal ofis taşıma), intercity moves (şehirlerarası), specialty items (piano, art, server racks).

Conversion target: phone call OR WhatsApp message OR submitted quote within 30 seconds of arrival.

## Product Purpose

Replace a generic, cluttered, distrust-inducing template site with a modern, cinematic, conversion-engineered website that signals craft, care, and competence — so a stressed visitor's gut tells them "these guys are different, my grandmother's piano is safe with them."

## Brand & Tone

**Mood**: Premium but warm. Confident, not loud. A high-end logistics company crossed with a boutique service.

**Voice in copy**: Short, direct, slightly editorial Turkish. Numerics and dates are protagonists. No exclamation marks. No "EN UCUZ FİYAT GARANTİ" energy. No emoji. No corporate jargon.

Examples:
- "2000'den bu yana, eviniz emin ellerde."
- "Eşyalarınız sigortalıdır. Hasar durumunda tam tazminat."
- "Tüm İstanbul'da hizmetinizdeyiz."

NOT:
- "Biz sizin en güvenilir taşıma çözümünüzüz!!!"
- "EN UCUZ! HEMEN ARA!"

## Anti-references

Do not look or feel like:
- Generic `evden eve nakliyat` template sites with cheesy stock photos of smiling families holding boxes.
- Loud red/yellow/orange "EN UCUZ" banner sites.
- Bootstrap-era carousel hero with a sliding truck image.
- Bloated corporate brochure sites with link-dump footers and clip-art icons.

## References (mood, not copy)

- **Stripe.com** — calmness, restraint, confident negative space.
- **Linear.app** — motion discipline, every animation has meaning.
- **Mercury.com** — typography as protagonist, editorial feel for a non-editorial product.
- **Apple product pages** — scroll choreography, sticky-section storytelling.
- **Goodfellas Movers (NYC)** — industry confidence done right.

## Strategic principles

1. **Trust is the entire job.** Every section either earns trust or it shouldn't be there.
2. **Mobile-first, always.** Design for a phone in dim light at 8pm Sunday. Desktop is the bonus surface.
3. **Numbers are protagonists.** "25+ yıl", "10.000+ taşıma", "39 ilçe" — set them huge, with care, with tabular figures.
4. **Real over stock.** Every photo placeholder must be replaceable with their actual trucks, team, and Istanbul rooftops. Never lean on stock imagery in production.
5. **Animation is state, not decoration.** A breathing element means "alive". A drawing line means "progress". A counter ticking means "the number changed".
6. **Conversion is two taps away from any pixel.** Floating WhatsApp + Phone cluster, persistent header CTA, in-section CTAs.
7. **Turkish only.** Copy never English. UI labels, error messages, alt text — all Turkish.
8. **Respect the visitor's anxiety.** No dark patterns. No countdown timers. No fake urgency. Earn trust by being calm.
9. **No `/blog`.** Old site had one — it was a SEO trap. Removed.

## Conversion architecture

- **Above-fold CTAs**: Teklif Al + Direct phone number. Visible in <1s.
- **Floating cluster (mobile)**: WhatsApp + Phone, always present, subtle pulse on entry.
- **Quote wizard (`/teklif-al`)**: multi-step, delightful, no price quoted (lead capture only) — "Ekibimiz 30 dakika içinde size dönecek."
- **Trust evidence layered every 1–2 viewports**: stats, fleet, testimonials, sigorta strip, Instagram social proof.
- **Final CTA section**: "Taşınmaya hazır mısınız?" — Teklif Al / WhatsApp / Phone, all huge.
