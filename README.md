# Yusufoğlu Nakliyat

Website redesign for an Istanbul moving company working since 2000, for home and
intercity moves: services, gallery, company pages, and an eight-step quote
wizard - districts on a map, a date, how full the home is - that ends in a
phone call, a WhatsApp message or a submitted quote.

The brief that shaped it, from PRODUCT.md: the website is the product, and its
job is to turn anxious visitors comparing three to five movers into a call
within thirty seconds. Premium but warm, short editorial Turkish, numbers and
dates up front, evidence of real work over promises. DESIGN.md carries the
design system.

Built solo, 2026. Next.js, TypeScript, Tailwind, Framer Motion.

Status: not deployed.
